# Support & feedback

Thanks for using ProfilePDF! 👋 Whether something broke, something could be better, or you just
want to say what you think, we'd love to hear from you. Every message is read by a real person
(the developer).

**Email: [kserjey2store@gmail.com](mailto:kserjey2store@gmail.com)**

## 💡 Ideas & feature requests

Missing a section in your PDF? Want another template or another language for the headings? Tell us. Requests from users decide what gets built next.

[Suggest a feature](mailto:kserjey2store@gmail.com?subject=ProfilePDF%20idea)

It helps to know:

- what you'd like ProfilePDF to do,
- what you'd use it for (for example "applying for jobs in the US", or "sharing my CV with a recruiter").

## 💬 General feedback

Love it? Something feels clunky? A sentence or two is plenty, and good or bad, it all helps.

[Send feedback](mailto:kserjey2store@gmail.com?subject=ProfilePDF%20feedback)

And if ProfilePDF saved you some time, a review on the Chrome Web Store helps other people find
it. Thank you!

## 🐞 Something not working?

First, try the quick fixes below. If those don't help, let us know and we'll look into it.

[Report a problem](mailto:kserjey2store@gmail.com?subject=ProfilePDF%20problem)

It helps a lot if you include:

- the error code from the popup, if there was one (for example `E-RENDER-FAILED`),
- the extension version (you'll find it in `chrome://extensions`),
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.

A description is all we need. Please don't attach the PDF or anyone's profile details.

### Quick fixes

**"Open a LinkedIn profile…"**: ProfilePDF works on profile pages, the ones whose address starts
with `linkedin.com/in/`. It doesn't work on the feed, search results or company pages.

**Some sections are missing from the PDF**: let ProfilePDF finish scrolling the page, and don't
press **Skip** early, because LinkedIn only loads sections as you scroll. If the automatic scrolling
stops (for example after you scroll up yourself), scroll to the bottom yourself. Also check that
you're signed in to LinkedIn.

**"The page did not respond."**: reload the profile page and click the icon again. This often
happens right after ProfilePDF was installed or updated.

**Some names or text are blank in the PDF**: ProfilePDF supports Latin (including Vietnamese),
Greek and Cyrillic scripts. Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Thai and others
aren't supported yet, so that text is left out of the PDF. When this happens, the popup says
which scripts were skipped, so check the PDF before sending it. If you need one, tell us and
it'll move up the list!
