# ProfilePDF: LinkedIn PDF Converter

Save any LinkedIn profile as a clean, multi-page PDF resume in one click. ProfilePDF is a Chrome extension that generates the LinkedIn PDF entirely in your browser.

## How to save a LinkedIn profile as PDF

1. Open a LinkedIn profile (`linkedin.com/in/…`) while signed in.
2. Click the ProfilePDF icon in the toolbar. Tip: pin it via the puzzle-piece menu.
3. Scroll the profile to the bottom once so every section loads.
4. Your PDF is saved to your Downloads folder.

## What you get

- Name, headline, location and photo
- Full experience with all roles at a company grouped together, plus descriptions and company logos
- Education and languages
- Selectable text and clean page breaks

## FAQ

### How do I save a LinkedIn profile as a PDF?

Open the profile (`linkedin.com/in/…`) while signed in, click the ProfilePDF icon, and scroll the profile to the bottom once so every section loads. The PDF is saved to your Downloads folder.

### How do I download my own LinkedIn profile as a PDF?

The same way: open your own profile and click the icon. ProfilePDF works on any `/in/` profile you can see in your browser, including yours.

### Does my LinkedIn data get uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is generated in your browser. There is no account, no upload and no server. See the [privacy policy](./privacy/).

### Why is the PDF missing something?

ProfilePDF reads the profile page as LinkedIn currently shows it, so a LinkedIn layout change can break a section. Text in Latin, Greek and Cyrillic scripts is supported. If something is wrong, tell us via [Support](./support/).

## Private by design

The profile and the PDF never leave your browser. There is no account, no upload and no server.
Anonymous diagnostics (error codes and counts, never profile content) help us notice when
LinkedIn changes break something, and you can turn them off in the extension's options.
Details are in the [privacy policy](./privacy/).
