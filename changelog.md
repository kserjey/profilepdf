# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Automatic scrolling: ProfilePDF now scrolls the profile to load every section for you. Scroll yourself or press **Skip** to use what has loaded.
- PDF layout fixes: page breaks, overlapping text and orphaned role markers.
- Diagnostics (still optional, still anonymous) now also note whether the page looked signed out, to help explain failed runs. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Save a LinkedIn profile as a PDF résumé, generated in the browser.
- Choose A4 or Letter and include or leave out your photo; your choices are remembered.
- Experience with multiple roles, descriptions and company logos; education; languages; photo.
- Full lists loaded from the profile's "Show all" pages.
- Works from a profile's "details" pages as well.
- Text in Latin (including Vietnamese), Greek and Cyrillic scripts. If other scripts can't be drawn, the popup says so.
- Optional anonymous diagnostics, which can be turned off in Options.
